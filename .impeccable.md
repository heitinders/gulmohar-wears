# Design context: Gulmohar Wears

Source: client master brief and corrective design brief, 21 September 2026.

Audience: people choosing bespoke Punjabi and Indian occasionwear, including
international customers discussing a remote fitting and worldwide delivery.
Use case: see real garments clearly, find an outfit or express a custom idea,
and start a useful WhatsApp or Instagram conversation with the Mohali atelier.
Tone: assured, warm, precise. A fashion editorial with the intimacy of a fitting.
Direction: Gulmohar, an atelier in bloom. Large color-faithful garment images,
full looks paired with meaningful details, fine editorial numbering, restrained
flower red on paper and oxblood. No decorative palace imagery or invented history.
Distinctive typography and varied open compositions. Glass is no longer the
organizing device. No anonymous stock, generated clothes, fake inventory or prices.
No em dashes, no hype. Preserve enquiry functionality and future GHL boundary.
