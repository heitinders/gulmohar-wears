# Rendered reference study

Reviewed 21 September 2026 in Chrome, desktop and phone viewports. Evidence is retained in screenshots/references. No reference photography, marks, copy or business history is reused.

- [Divani home](https://divanicouture.com/): garments occupy most of the opening composition. Restrained navigation and small labels allow richly detailed photographs to establish the brand. Gulmohar translates this into a full silhouette/detail pair and short adjacent copy.
- [Divani Zardoz](https://divanicouture.com/pages/zardoz-project): oversized visual chapters move between atmospheric scenes and close craft views. Warm light and deep backgrounds give the sequence weight; varied image scale prevents a card-grid rhythm. Gulmohar uses its own oxblood/paper palette, a staggered current edit and a detail/wider-view pairing.
- At phone widths, imagery remains the central event rather than becoming a decorative thumbnail. Gulmohar preserves an identifiable complete outfit in the opening, with the headline below and visible image entries in the collection index.
- Gulmohar has no verified workshop photographs or documented multigenerational heritage in the supplied material. Its closer-look chapter describes visible embroidery and drape only; Divani's craft history is not borrowed.

Apple remains a usability reference: readable navigation, clear actions and predictable interaction. The rescue brief explicitly replaced glass as the site's organising visual treatment.
