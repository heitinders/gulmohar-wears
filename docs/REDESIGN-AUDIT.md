# Corrective audit

The existing running page was captured at 1440px and 390px before changes.
Screenshots are in screenshots/before. Verdict: the foundation reads like a
generic design-system demonstration, not a fashion atelier.

| Affected area | Concrete weakness | Change |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Opening | Brand JPEG fills the image role, with no clothes in the first screen | Real garment campaign occupying most of the opening |
| Navigation | Identity, Details and Experience describe the styleguide | Collections, Custom and Atelier lead to real pages |
| Main narrative | Palette, typography and UI-state demos replace customer content | Current edit, outfit details, personal process and invitation |
| Composition | Equal padding and repeated split sections create a long monotonous page | Unequal photographic spreads, a compact index and varied chapter intervals |
| Typography | Cormorant/Inter with italic second lines repeats an unconsidered template | Render two studies against the same garment and choose a fitted display/text system |

Useful elements retained: encoded enquiry messages, review before WhatsApp,
Instagram route, native dialog behavior, contact source and GHL-neutral model.

## Reference method

Divani home and Zardoz were opened and captured in rendered desktop and mobile
Chromium. Screenshots are in screenshots/references. No reference imagery is
used in the product build. The homepage popup was dismissed for a clear view.
Zardoz gives its workshop film the entire opening, then cuts immediately to a
close embroidery view beside a dark text field. Small tracked labels sit above
more expressive headings. Home places garments at architectural scale behind
compact navigation. Translate the image scale and wider-to-detail rhythm, not
the decorative border motifs, heavy sepia cast or brand history.

Gulmohar has model shoot assets. These cannot establish workshop activity.
The redesign will use accurate garment details and omit a workshop-photo claim.
