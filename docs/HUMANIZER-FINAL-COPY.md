ORDERING WITH US / GULMOHAR WEARS

What to send us before we start

Your first message can be simple. Tell us the occasion date and where you need the outfit delivered. If you have saved a few photographs, send those too. We can use them to discuss the design, fit and timing.

Tell us which occasion

Let us know what you are dressing for and what you feel comfortable wearing. If there are several wedding events, say which one this outfit is for. A suit for a daytime ceremony may be quite different from what you have in mind for the evening.

Show us what you like

Send your references with a short note about each one. You might like the neckline but want a different sleeve, or love the colour and prefer a simpler border. Those comments help more than a photograph on its own.

Include the date and delivery location

Tell us when the outfit needs to arrive and which city it is going to. We will check the making time and shipping options with you before you order.

Check the details before paying

Ask about the fabric, embroidery technique and care instructions, and make sure the measurements and price are agreed. A reference is a useful starting point. The final order should spell out what we are making for you.

Start your conversation

## /journal/look-at-the-whole-outfit

← All atelier notes

CHOOSING YOUR OUTFIT / GULMOHAR WEARS

What to look for in an outfit photograph

Start with the full outfit, then look at the close-ups. The wider photograph shows the shape; the details show the neckline, sleeves, hem and dupatta. If something is hard to see, ask us for another view or a short video.

Look at the full length

Notice where the upper piece ends, the width of the trousers and how the dupatta falls. A close-up of embroidery can be lovely, but it will not tell you how the whole outfit looks.

Follow the embroidery

Look for details that repeat on the sleeves, hem or dupatta border. Each of our product galleries keeps the full view and close-ups of that same suit together, so you can compare them.

Check the colour with us

The same fabric can look different in sunlight, indoors and on your screen. If you need a particular shade, ask us to confirm it and send another view before you decide.

Ask about fabric and care

A photograph cannot reliably tell you the fabric composition, how the embroidery was made or how to care for the outfit. We can discuss those details, along with making time, when you enquire.

Start your conversation
