# Gulmohar, an atelier in bloom

Implemented redesign, September 2026. The corrective brief supersedes the earlier glass foundation.

## Composition

The garment leads. The opening pairs a portrait film from C0094 with DSC07089's embroidered surface, on oxblood. Mobile places the same pair above the headline. The current edit staggers a dominant fuchsia film with a smaller blue look. The collection index gives keyboard focus the same preview behaviour as hover; phones show persistent thumbnails. The detail chapter pairs two views of one outfit. Open layouts, crisp edges and restrained rules replace glass cards and pills.

The compact header and mobile menu arrange the supplied flowers beside the original Gurmukhi lettering and gold WEARS line. CSS windows and a contour clip isolate those parts of the original JPEG; no logo lettering is retyped. The complete artwork, including the tagline, remains in the footer. The source image is unchanged. No generated garments or invented history.

## Typography

Bodoni Moda 400 regular and italic for display; Manrope 400 and 500 for readable text. Self-hosted WOFF2 through next/font/local. Both carry SIL Open Font License 1.1; license copies are in public/fonts/licenses. Newsreader is confined to the alternative study.

The rendered `/design-studies` compares Bodoni Moda's precise fashion direction with Newsreader's warmer literary direction, using identical copy and DSC07053. Bodoni was selected for its crisp contrast against the richly detailed clothing. Uppercase opening lines move into an italic occasion; inner pages use quieter sentence case. Phone headline breaks are intentionally different.

Display 36–102px, section titles 38–80px, body 13–17px depending on role, compact navigation 12–13px. Small numbered captions are supporting metadata only. Inputs stay 16px to avoid mobile zoom. Reading measures remain constrained; headings never sit across embroidery.

## Layout and interaction

Content width 1600px maximum, fluid 20–80px gutters. Desktop editorial chapters vary from 64px to 128px spacing. Mobile uses 32–64px by content. Specific image ratios and focal points are deliberate exceptions to a universal grid. The full product gallery retains the original portrait ratio. Most transitions are 300ms, disabled under reduced motion. Silent editorial films play only while visible, with a persistent pause control. Reduced motion, data saving and slow connections default to stills. No parallax or entrance-animation dependencies.

Actions are crisp rectangles or underlined links. Navigation is 80px on desktop and 72px on phones, plus its 1px rule. A warm paper surface, serif links and oxblood enquiry action tie it to the editorial pages. Collections opens a keyboard-accessible panel with category links and actual shoot photographs. Escape and outside clicks dismiss it. The mobile menu has a garment detail and direct contact links. There is no separate announcement strip. User-requested floating contact shortcuts pair a rose Instagram orb above a green WhatsApp orb, with raised edges and soft shadows for a 3D finish. Instagram opens a DM; the footer icon links to the profile. Each has a label on hover and keyboard focus, a compact 58px mobile target, safe-area offsets and an outfit-specific message on product routes. The pair hides while the mobile menu is open or a phone form field is focused. The footer reserves clearance. There is no idle animation; reduced motion removes movement. The mobile menu is a native dialog with focus containment, Escape dismissal, focus restoration and a locked background. Product thumbnails are real buttons with aria-pressed. The product detail film loads on request and uses native controls. Home films defer media loading until visible and the page has loaded; manual pause persists through scrolling. All motion retains a still fallback. Enquiry forms validate, prepare a readable message, then open WhatsApp only when the customer chooses.

## Content boundaries

Only photographed features are described. Fabric composition, manufacturing technique, price, availability and delivery timing are confirmed in conversation. Three descriptive product records correspond to three actual outfits. Lehengas remain custom enquiries without a fabricated product image. No workshop imagery was supplied; model photographs are identified as garment imagery.

There are no payments, live CRM writes, trackers, fake purchase confirmations or assumed operational terms. Integration boundaries are documented in docs/INTEGRATIONS.md. The preview remains noindex pending a confirmed production domain and launch content.

## Customer voice

The Humanizer skill guides customer copy: describe the clothing and the next step plainly, vary sentence length and avoid stacked slogans, vague claims and forced groups of three. Use we/you when the atelier is speaking, preserve verified facts and keep practical order information specific. See docs/HUMANIZER-REVIEW.md for draft, audit and final-copy evidence.
